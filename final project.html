Your Health, Your Wealth

Reliable, local health insights custom-tailored for Sierra Leonean families.

Free Health Care Initiative

Completely free clinical treatment is provided under government programs for pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers, and children under the age of 5 across public clinics.

Malaria Intervention

Malaria is fully preventable. Sleep under treated bed nets nightly, keep surrounding water from pooling, and head directly to a clinic for a checkup if fever starts.

Clean Hygiene & Sanitation

Prevent stomach sickness and cholera. Use running water and hand soap consistently, and purify drinking water with local chlorine treatments or boiling methods.

Our Vision & Goals

Our mission is simple: to expand healthcare literacy and information access to every citizen of Sierra Leone.

Eliminating Communication Barriers

We strive to provide simple, bilingual summaries of important medical guidelines so that language, reading barriers, or high data-costs do not stand in the way of safety.

Supporting Local Infrastructure

We support national health priorities set by the Ministry of Health by aligning our community-facing tips directly with national medical frameworks.

About the Initiative

Welbodi na Gentri is an independent, non-commercial public health educational portal. Translating literally to "Health is Wealth", our focus remains on providing clear information.

By offering small, high-contrast, data-efficient content, we help ensure families from urban Freetown districts to rural provincial villages can navigate core medical resources easily.

Important Medical Disclaimer

All details published on this page are intended exclusively for educational overview. They are not direct medical advice. Always consult with certified medical experts at an established health clinic for all diagnoses.
